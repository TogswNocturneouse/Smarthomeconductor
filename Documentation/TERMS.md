# Terms of Use Draft

Last updated: 2026-07-25

SmartHomeConductor is currently pre-release software. This draft requires legal review
and completion with the operator's identity, governing law, support terms, and billing
terms before paid distribution.

The product must not be represented as a life-safety, medical, security-alarm, fire,
emergency, or guaranteed energy-control system. Users remain responsible for device
installation, electrical safety, manufacturer instructions, network security, and
maintaining safe manual control.

Supported models, firmware, operating systems, and connection routes must be listed
clearly. Experimental routes must be labeled. Subscription limits, renewal, taxes,
cancellation, downgrade, refunds, data export, and deletion must be stated before
purchase.

Remote support may act only within explicit authorization and must be audited. No term
may authorize silent access to cameras, microphones, locks, alarms, or private home
data.

This document is a product draft, not legal advice.
