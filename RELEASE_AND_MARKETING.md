# Release And Marketing Pack

## Release Path

1. TestFlight private beta
   - Best for the first 20-100 real homes.
   - Validate discovery, manual inventory, Apple Home import, privacy language, and assistant safety.
   - Place: https://appstoreconnect.apple.com

2. App Store soft launch
   - Countries: start with one or two markets where support load is manageable.
   - Category: Lifestyle or Utilities.
   - Positioning: elegant private smart-home command center for mixed-device homes.
   - Place: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/submitting/

3. Subscriber launch
   - Add subscriptions only after the gateway, knowledge base, and feedback loop are stable.
   - Keep local-only inventory and Apple Home import useful without a subscription.
   - Place: App Store Connect auto-renewable subscriptions.

4. Partner and community channels
   - Home Assistant community users.
   - Smart-home installers.
   - iOS/Mac power users with mixed Tapo, Xiaomi, Samsung, Midea, Electrolux, Shelly, Matter, and HomeKit setups.

## Pricing

Use fair, transparent pricing.

Free:
- Manual inventory.
- Local device scan.
- Apple Home import.
- Local-only assistant guidance.
- Up to 20 learned records.

Plus:
- EUR 4.99/month or EUR 39.99/year.
- Persistent assistant history.
- Larger home memory.
- Cloud manual search and citations.
- Automation proposals.

Pro Home:
- EUR 9.99/month or EUR 79.99/year.
- Multi-home profiles.
- Advanced diagnostics.
- More document storage.
- Priority model and gateway usage.

Installer:
- EUR 19.99/month per installer seat.
- Client handoff packs.
- Exportable home reports.
- Faster support workflows.

Launch offer:
- First month free for Plus.
- Annual plan at about two months free.
- No fake discounts, no hidden control lock-in.

Customer-friendly rules:
- Keep manual inventory, delete/export, privacy, and safety controls free.
- Do not charge for fixing broken account connections.
- Do not sell "complete brand support" until the connector is actually authorized and tested.
- Give a clear local-only mode for users who do not want cloud AI.
- Make cancellation and downgrade obvious inside the subscription screen.

## Subscription Gate

Never paywall basic safety, export, delete, or privacy controls. Subscription should pay for expensive services: hosted retrieval, gateway inference, sync, diagnostics, and support.

## Brand Voice

Dark luxury, technical confidence, privacy-first. Avoid goofy mascots, exaggerated smart-home magic, and claims of complete support before connectors are actually authorized.
